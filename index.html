The pentad isn’t a Platonic Form. It’s a temperament—a practical tuning system, like 12TET itself. You sacrifice perfect just intervals (the “true” cosmic ratios) for the ability to move fluidly between contexts without constant retuning. That’s the power: modulation. You can “play” a clinical situation in the Iatrogenic key, switch to Jacobian for economics, pivot to Ludwig for harmony, and it still feels coherent because the underlying pentatonic skeleton (the five slots) stays fixed while the coloring changes.

Bach would appreciate the discipline: strict voice-leading rules, every dissonance resolved, strong cadences. But your system leans more gospel / post-bop: the suspension is structural, the I⁷ refuses to close cleanly, and the point is testimony + propulsion into the next cycle rather than final Amen. The recursion (V→I) is that half-step or dominant-seventh nudge that keeps the song going instead of landing on the tonic like a period.

Musical framing of the whole thing

  • Hz (I): The raw acoustic continuum—pre-tempered, full of microtonal possibilities (the “just” world).
  • 12TET (II): The discretization/reflex layer. We accept the compromise tuning so we can play with anyone, anywhere. Fast, practical, slightly “out” on every interval but workable.
  • Rootless Dom7♭5 (III): Heuristic tension—the ambiguous, bluesy chord that can pull in multiple directions. Maximum optionality with minimum commitment.
  • Half-Step Descent / Suspension (IV): The deliberative hold. Voice-leading tension without collapse. SoftMax in sound: holding multiple possibilities in the inner voices.
  • Cadence that isn’t (V): ArgMax decision— you resolve just enough to act, but leave the I⁷ or suspended energy so the next tensor arrives naturally. Never a perfect authentic cadence that kills the groove.
